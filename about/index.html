Full Stack Engineer

Zephyr Flanigan

I'm a software engineer based in New York, working across the full stack, though I naturally gravitate toward front-end development. I enjoy the challenge of solving technical problems, but what keeps me coming back is building something people can actually interact with. There's something satisfying about watching an idea become a real product that someone can see, use, and enjoy.

Outside of software, you'll usually find me knitting, arranging music, or in the kitchen baking something new. I love to take things that are not much on their own and turn them into something whole. A ball of yarn turns into a scarf, sugar and flour into a cake, notes on a scale into complex harmonies, and of course, lines of code into enaging and intuitive platforms.

No matter the medium, I always have the same goal: to create something that invites users to explore and connect with one another, whether it’s through a screen, a song, or a freshly baked cookie.
