Craft over speed

I'd rather take an extra day to do something right than spend two weeks undoing a shortcut. Good software is slow to build and slow to break.

Curiosity first

I ask a lot of questions before writing a line of code. Understanding the problem deeply almost always makes the solution simpler.

Collaboration as default

The best work I've done has always been with other people. I prefer to pair, review, and discuss openly rather than work in isolation.
